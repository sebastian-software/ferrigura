The corpus in `tests/corpus/paths.json` is generated by `tools/port/path-bounds.ts` from Chromium 151's
own `getBBox()`, with the `known-differences.json` list of the paths the port does not yet match.

The sine and cosine of a rotation (`SkMatrix::setRotate` and the arc loop) come from one `__sincosf_stret` call
on macOS (`skia.rs`, `sin_cos`), which is the form that the corpus matches bit for bit. The other platforms take
`sin` and `cos` separately, and the corpus test is ignored there.
