Ferrigura's checks compare every drawing with Mermaid's to within 0.05 px. This page lets you look instead: ten everyday diagrams of medium size, each drawn from the same text by both. Ferrigura is on the left, Mermaid on the right; on a narrow screen, Ferrigura comes first.

## How the drawings were made

Both sides are the published packages, <Versions />, and nothing is touched up afterwards. Mermaid draws in headless Chromium; Ferrigura draws without a browser. Both get the configuration that the geometry check gives them, because Mermaid's defaults would change the look rather than the drawing: labels as SVG text instead of HTML, Ferrigura's bundled face and the `redux-color` theme, which is one of Mermaid's own.

<SharedConfig />

Flowcharts are drawn in Ferrigura's `mermaid` mode, which keeps Mermaid's edge routing; the default `adaptive` mode is shown on the [homepage](/#flowchart-mode). Ferrigura's drawing is the SVG file as a site would ship it, with its fonts inside. Mermaid's drawing stands in this page, which supplies the face. Each figure links both files, and the diagram text opens below it.

## Where they differ

The positions, sizes and texts agree in all ten. Two diagrams differ in their colors, which the geometry check does not compare:

- **Class diagram.** Mermaid gives each class its own color from the theme; Ferrigura draws every class in black and white.
- **State diagram.** Ferrigura fills the note dark, so its text cannot be read, and fills the choice and the final states where Mermaid draws them hollow.

## Render them yourself

`scripts/render-comparison.mjs` in the [homepage's sources](https://github.com/sebastian-software/ferrigura/tree/main/homepage) writes these files with `pnpm comparison:write`, and the site's checks render them again and fail when a file no longer matches.
