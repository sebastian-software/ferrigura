# P3 renderers: how a renderer task works

The working rules of [M6-RENDERERS.md](./M6-RENDERERS.md) and [P2-RENDERERS.md](./P2-RENDERERS.md) apply to the P3 renderer tasks: how a renderer is checked with `pnpm port:geometry <type>`, the math of `ferrigura_jsmath`, the text boxes, the fonts the harness maps, the worktree setup, the gates and the report. The research is in `docs-port/<type>/NOTES.md` (section 6, the renderer) and the decisions in [P3-PLAN.md](./P3-PLAN.md). What P3 changes and adds:

- **The contract** is [typed-views.md](./typed-views.md). Each P3 stub already takes `&Context<'_, Model, Section>`, with the schema's section of its type (`AgentflowDiagramConfig`, `CynefinDiagramConfig`, `RadarDiagramConfig`, `RailroadDiagramConfig`, `TreeViewDiagramConfig`, `UsecaseDiagramConfig`, `VennDiagramConfig`, `WardleyDiagramConfig`), and its facade arm builds that context. A renderer reads its section, `input.globals` and the theme as tokens (`input.theme.light.<field>`, `input.theme.dark` for `colorScheme: auto`); `common::style::base_style_sheet` takes the `ThemeView`.
- **A section of its own**: where the renderer reads more than its schema section (agentflow and use case read the unified renderer's sections, `unified::Section`), it gets a small handwritten section type next to the renderer, as mindmap's `MindmapSection`, and the facade arm builds it. No renderer takes the whole `MermaidConfig`: `context::tests::no_renderer_takes_the_whole_configuration` fails when one does.
- **Theme tokens a type needs**: a variable or a group that `ThemeTokens` does not have yet (the `radar` and `treeView` groups of `themeVariables`, a type's own variables) is added to the list in `crates/ferrigura-model/src/theme/tokens.rs` (`field = "jsName": token | family(N, first) | group(Type)`), in a block of its own for the type at the end of the list, so that parallel tasks merge cleanly. The equivalence test in `ferrigura-render` (`variables::tests`) covers new tokens and families as they are; a new group gets its own case there, as `xyChart` and `packet` have.
- **Globals** are a closed list (`context::Globals`); a task that needs another top-level option says so in its report.
- **Layout engines**: venn uses `ferrigura-venn` (exact against the library on 447 captured cases); agentflow and use case lay out with ELK Layered through the unified renderer. Swimlanes is its own milestone and is not a P3 renderer task.
- **Shared code**: agentflow (the `flowGroup` cluster kind) and use case (its shapes) change `crates/ferrigura-render/src/unified/`, which eight other types share. Keep those changes additive: new shapes and clusters in new files, new entries in the registries, no change to what an existing shape or cluster draws. List every change outside the type's own module in the report.
- **Existing output stays the same**: a task must not change any SVG of the 24 types ported before P3. The check is the corpus comparison of all 2336 `.mmd` files under three configurations and four colour schemes, as in [typed-views.md](./typed-views.md) (the task brief names the script): 0 differing renders outside the P3 fixtures; the renders of the type's own fixtures are expected to change.
- **Status**: the coordinator updates `docs-port/STATUS.md` and the status tables after the merges; a task does not commit them.
- **Symbol face**: the arrows of cynefin's subtitles are set in the bundled symbol face (`FontFamily::Symbols`, decision 1 of 2026-10-18); text in other uncovered blocks keeps its harness exclusion.
